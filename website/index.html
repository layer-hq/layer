Your screen ⇋ your LLM.

Layer lives in your Mac’s notch and connects what’s on your screen with the LLM of your choice.

Ask questions, understand what you’re looking at, or get things done without breaking your flow.

Layer is fast

... so you can do your thing without waiting.

Layer is built to stay out of your way. From keyboard shortcuts to the interface itself, every interaction is designed to get you from thought to result as quickly as possible.

Layer is open source

... so you can exactly what happens to your data.

Bring your own LLM keys, inspect the code, understand how your context is handled, and decide for yourself where to draw the line between privacy and convenience.

Layer is malleable

... so you can work your LLM the way you want.

Everyone works with LLMs differently. Customize your shortcuts, choose your models and LLM gateway, and shape Layer around the way you work.
